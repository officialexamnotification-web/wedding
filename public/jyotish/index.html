About the Jyotish tools

What information does a birth chart show?

The chart report includes birth details, ascendant, Moon sign, Nakshatra and Pada, sidereal planetary placements, houses and Panchanga details.

How does kundali matching work here?

The matching tool compares both charts using eight traditional Ashtakoota factors, with a total score out of 36. Treat this as a traditional reference, not a certain prediction or a substitute for personal guidance.

Can I view the results in Hindi and save a PDF?

The tools provide English and Hindi result labels, and supported reports can be printed or saved as PDF from your browser.

Are birth details stored or sent to the site?

Chart calculations run in your browser. Place search uses an external place-search provider to resolve a selected location; see the Privacy Policy for details.
