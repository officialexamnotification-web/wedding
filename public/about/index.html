ABOUT THE STUDIO

A thoughtful way to invite

Learn about Nimantran Royale, a design studio for personal Indian digital invitations and celebration cards.

Nimantran Royale is an online invitation design studio for weddings and family celebrations. It brings editable card layouts, Indian-inspired artwork, Hindi and English text tools, photo frames, venue details and digital export options into one place.

Designed around the occasion

Start with a wedding suite, a Griha Pravesh card, a Sangeet or Haldi invitation, a birthday design, a Godh Bharai card or an anniversary invitation. Each design can be personalized in the browser with the details that matter to your family.

Made to personalize and share

Use the studio to edit text, colours, images and decorative elements. Depending on the design and your device, you can export a still image, a multi-page PDF or an animated invitation video. You choose what to send and to whom.

Our approach

We aim to make invitation design approachable while respecting the meaning and variety of Indian celebrations. Always review names, dates, venue information, translations and any religious text before sharing or printing.
