Privacy Policy

Read how Nimantran Royale handles browser-saved invitation projects, uploaded content and optional AI wording tools.

Last updated: 22 October 2026. This policy explains the information involved when you use Nimantran Royale. The site may change as features are added, so review this page periodically.

Invitation projects and browser storage

The current editor saves projects in your browser’s local storage on the device you use. Anyone with access to that browser profile may be able to access those saved projects. Clearing browser storage can remove them. Keep your own exported backup of important work.

Images, audio and other uploads

Files you select are used by the editor to build or export your design. Do not upload content you do not have permission to use, or sensitive information you do not want included in an invitation. Exported files are created for you to download or share.

Optional AI wording

If you choose an AI wording feature, the text you submit may be sent to Google’s Gemini service using the API configuration available to the application. Avoid sending sensitive personal information through AI tools. Google’s handling of that request is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy information.

Third-party services and sharing

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. When you choose map, WhatsApp, or device-share features, your browser opens the relevant third-party service. Those services process information under their own policies. Nimantran does not send an invitation to your contacts without your action.

Your choices and questions

You can remove browser-saved projects by clearing the site’s local storage in your browser. For privacy questions, contact support@invitationcards.site.
