Privacy Policy

Learn how Nimantran Royale handles browser-saved invitation projects, uploaded content, sharing and local wording suggestions.

Last updated: 22 October 2026. This notice describes how information is handled when you use Nimantran Royale’s invitation editor and website.

Projects saved on your device

Saved invitation projects are stored in this browser on the device you use. Project text, design settings and media included in a saved project may be accessible to people who can use that browser profile. You can remove saved projects in the editor or clear this site’s browser storage. Keep an exported copy of work you want to retain.

Uploads and editing

Images, audio and video you select are used by the browser-based editor to create your design and exports. Wording suggestions are assembled locally from text included with the website. Avoid adding information you do not want visible on an invitation. When you download or share an export, the destination you choose may process that file under its own terms and privacy notice.

Wording suggestions

Invitation wording suggestions come from copy included with the website and are assembled in your browser from the details you enter. They are not generated by a remote AI service. Review all names, dates, translations and religious wording before use.

Website hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted by Vercel, which may process technical request information to deliver and secure the site. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you choose a map, WhatsApp or device-share feature, your browser may contact or open that service. Those providers handle information under their own policies. The editor does not send invitations to your contacts unless you choose a sharing action.

Your choices and questions

You can remove saved projects through the editor or clear this site’s browser storage. For privacy questions, contact support@invitationcards.site.
